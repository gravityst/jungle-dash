# Third-party assets

## Nature Kit — Kenney

* Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
* Author: Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
* Licence: **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)** — public domain, no attribution
  required, free for commercial use. Full text in `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
* Version 2.1, downloaded 2026-09-17.

Only a curated subset of the 329-model kit is included here — the palms, trees,
bushes, rocks, logs, stumps, flowers and grass this game actually uses. They
appear both as scenery and as the log, boulder and tree OBSTACLES.

The models are used as geometry only: every surface is recoloured into this
project's own palette at build time (see `_kenney_collect()` in
`tools/build_scenes.gd`), so none of Kenney's materials ship in the game.

CC0 asks for nothing, but crediting Kenney costs nothing either and the work is
worth it.

## Everything else

The character, the track itself, the landmarks, the vines and low branches, the
stone idol, the shaders and all the sounds are original to this project and
generated from code in `tools/` and `shaders/`.
